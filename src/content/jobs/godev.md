### Professional Go Developer

okTurtles is looking for a strong [Go](https://go.dev) developer.

If you have a researcher's mind that seeks the truth, this project is for you.

#### Requirements

- 5+ years of experience working on backends
- 1+ year of go-specific experience
- Strong understanding of relational databases (Postgresql, etc.)
- Able to join the team on video calls to discuss work once per week (if needed)
- Able to share with us a portfolio of your best work
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

#### Note on AI

While we encourage our developers to use AI to improve their efficiency, we **never** blindly accept the output of AI code at okTurtles. It must **always** be thoroughly reviewed by a professional.

If the AI can write better code than you can without AI assistance, this job isn't for you, please do not waste our time.

We want someone who can write better backend code than the best AIs out there. Someone who knows how to use AI to their advantage, but not someone who uses it as a crutch to compensate for their own shortcomings.

We are not hiring an AI. We are hiring a human professional: someone who already possesses the necessary skills to get the job done without AI.

#### Instructions

To apply, follow these instructions:

1. Create a message with a link to your online portfolio, resumé, and why we should hire you. The message must also include two links to source code that you wrote entirely yourself that you are most proud of for us to review.
2. Email this message to [hi@okturtles.org](mailto:hi@okturtles.org).
3. Install [Keybase](https://keybase.io/) and send a complete copy of that message to [https://keybase.io/greg](https://keybase.io/greg). This ensures we hear from you in case there's an email delivery problem.

Please make sure to do all 3 steps if you'd like to hear back from us.
