### Grant Writer

okTurtles Foundation seeks a grant writer who understands our mission of supporting beneficial decentralization technologies through research, education, and development.

If you have a passion for impactful causes and the skills to craft compelling proposals that win funding, we'd love to hear from you.

#### Requirements

- Strong technical writing experience
- Strong knowledge of funding sources, including foundations, government grants, and corporate philanthropy relevant to technology and open-source projects
- Excellent writing, editing, and research skills with the ability to tailor proposals to specific funders
- Able to join the team on video calls to discuss work once per week (if needed)
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

#### Nice to Haves

- If you have prior experience writing grants specifically, this will count in your favor

#### Instructions

To apply:

1. Create a message with the following information:
    - A link to samples of your best technical writing, written by you, not AI.
    - Examples of previous experience either doing grant writing or marketing.
    - At least one paragraph explaining what it is about our work that you find most interesting and why.
    - At least one paragraph on why we should hire you instead of somebody else.
2. Email this message to [hi@okturtles.org](mailto:hi@okturtles.org).
3. Install [Keybase](https://keybase.io/) and send a complete copy of that message to [https://keybase.io/greg](https://keybase.io/greg). This ensures we hear from you in case there's an email delivery problem.

Please make sure to do all 3 steps above if you'd like to hear back from us.
